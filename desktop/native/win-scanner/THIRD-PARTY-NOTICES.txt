Paperwing scanner helpers for Windows (paperwing-scan.exe, paperwing-twain32.exe)
Copyright (c) 2026 Rohan Vijaykumar. Part of Paperwing, licensed under the
GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (see LICENSE.txt next to Paperwing).

The helpers use the following open-source libraries, shipped unmodified as
separate DLLs in this folder. Their scanner-handling approach follows NAPS2
(https://github.com/cyanfish/naps2) by Ben Olden-Cooligan.

TWAIN drivers are reached through the TWAIN Data Source Manager that scanner
drivers install with Windows (twaindsm.dll, or Windows' own twain_32.dll);
Paperwing doesn't ship it.
